I am a PhD student in Electrical Engineering at Texas A&M University, advised by Prof. Byung-Jun Yoon in the BioMLSP Lab. My primary research interest is in machine-learning methods for drug design and molecular discovery, with an emphasis on reliable prediction and design under uncertainty.

## Education

- **PhD in Electrical Engineering**, Texas A&M University, College Station, TX. Advisor: Prof. Byung-Jun Yoon. January 2021 - Present.
- **BSc in Electrical and Electronic Engineering**, Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology, Dhaka, Bangladesh. February 2013 - September 2017.


## Research themes

- **Machine learning for drug design:** generative molecular design, uncertainty-guided model adaptation, and active learning.
- **Protein property prediction:** protein language models and structure-based methods.
